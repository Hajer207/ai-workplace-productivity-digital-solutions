# Responsible AI Use

## Accuracy

I compare AI-generated names, dates, numbers, requirements, and decisions with the original source. Missing information is labeled rather than guessed.

## Accountability

I remain responsible for the final content. AI supports drafting and organization, but it does not own the analysis or the decision.

## Confidentiality

I do not enter real confidential project documents, client information, credentials, contracts, or internal security details into unapproved AI tools. This portfolio uses fictional or anonymized information only.

## Privacy

I remove personal identifiers unless they are necessary, permitted, and processed in an approved environment. Real employee, customer, or stakeholder information is not included in this repository.

## Bias

I review summaries, prioritization, and recommendations for unsupported assumptions or unfair treatment. I ask for evidence and alternative options before accepting an AI recommendation.

## Human Oversight

A qualified person reviews important requirements, communications, risks, and decisions. AI does not make the final project, legal, privacy, security, or people-related decision.

## Green / Amber / Red Classification

| Information Item | Classification | Reason | Required Handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public project template with fictional examples | Green | It contains no real personal, confidential, or organizational information | May be used in an approved AI tool after a quick review |
| Anonymized internal meeting notes with project context | Amber | Even without names, the context may still reveal internal plans or risks | Use only in an organization-approved tool, minimize details, and review before sharing |
| Real customer records, employee IDs, passwords, contracts, or security details | Red | It contains personal, confidential, contractual, or security-sensitive information | Do not enter it into a general AI tool; follow organizational policy and use authorized systems only |

## Portfolio Safety Confirmation

- All examples are fictional or taken from general course scenarios.
- No real client, employee, or organizational records are included.
- No passwords, credentials, contracts, private contact information, or security details are included.
- All AI-assisted content was checked and edited by the trainee.
