# Professional Writing Example

This example uses the four-step writing workflow: **Draft, Verify, Refine, and Human Sign-off**.

## 1. AI-Generated Draft

**Subject: Important Project Delay**

Hello Team,

Unfortunately, the project has been delayed because of technical problems. The deadline is now September 25, 2026 instead of September 15, 2026. This may affect several activities, so please adjust your work and contact the technical team if you have concerns.

Regards,  
Hajar

## 2. Fact Verification

I checked the draft against the confirmed scenario:

| Item | Verification Result |
|---|---|
| Original deadline | Confirmed: September 15, 2026 |
| New deadline | Confirmed: September 25, 2026 |
| Reason | Confirmed only as a technical delay |
| Impact on several activities | Not confirmed; removed |
| Contacting the technical team | Not specified; replaced with the project channel |

## 3. Tone Refinement

I changed **“Important Project Delay”** to **“Project Timeline Update”** to make the subject neutral and professional. I removed **“Unfortunately”** and **“technical problems”** because they sounded negative and less precise. I also removed the unconfirmed claim about several activities being affected.

## 4. Human Review and Sign-off

I confirmed that the final version:

- includes both dates;
- gives the confirmed reason;
- does not blame a person or team;
- does not include unverified impacts; and
- is concise and suitable for the project team.

**Human approval status:** Approved by the trainee for this fictional course example.

## 5. Final Version

**Subject: Project Timeline Update - New Deadline**

Hello Team,

Please note that the project deadline has been updated from September 15, 2026 to September 25, 2026 due to a technical delay.

Please update your plans to reflect the new date. If this change affects a confirmed dependency, kindly raise it through the project channel.

Best regards,  
Hajar
