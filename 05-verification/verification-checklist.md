# Verification Checklist

## My Personal Checklist

Before using or sharing AI-generated workplace content, I will:

1. Identify the type of content, intended audience, and level of risk.
2. Compare names, dates, numbers, decisions, and quotations with the original source.
3. Check that uncertainty, assumptions, and missing information are clearly labeled.
4. Confirm that the AI did not invent owners, deadlines, status, causes, or impacts.
5. Check that the format, tone, and level of detail fit the intended audience.
6. Remove or anonymize confidential, private, or sensitive information.
7. Record important corrections and obtain human approval before final use.

## Worked Example

### Output Selected

The first AI-generated meeting brief in [Section 3](../03-information-workflow/information-processing-example.md).

### Application of the Checklist

| Check | What I Tested | Finding | Action Taken |
|---|---|---|---|
| Content and audience | Project brief for a project manager | Correct format, but some statements were too certain | Rewrote the brief to preserve uncertainty |
| Names, dates, and numbers | Priya, Monday, Thursday, and 200 users | These matched the source | Kept them unchanged |
| Decisions vs. open questions | Press push | AI incorrectly treated it as planned | Changed it to an open decision |
| Missing owners and deadlines | FAQ owner and onboarding deadline | AI invented or implied values | Replaced them with `[Not Specified]` and retained "before launch" only where supported |
| Status and impact | "In progress" and launch impacts | Not stated in the source | Removed unsupported status and impact claims |
| Sensitive information | Names and project details | The course scenario is fictional; no real confidential data is included | Confirmed safe for this portfolio |
| Human approval | Final brief | Corrections were reviewed | Marked final version as trainee-reviewed |

## Verification Result

The original AI output was **not ready to use** because it invented ownership, deadlines, and decisions. After comparing it with the raw notes, I corrected the unsupported statements and produced a final brief that accurately distinguishes confirmed facts, pending approvals, open questions, and missing information.

**Final decision:** Approved for inclusion as a fictional, trainee-reviewed course example.
