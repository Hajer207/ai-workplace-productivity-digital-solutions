# Before and After Prompt Example

## Before - Weak Prompt

> Write an email about the timeline update.

## Initial Result

The initial result would probably be generic because the prompt does not explain the audience, old deadline, new deadline, reason, tone, or required format. The AI might also guess missing project details.

## After - Structured Prompt

### C.A.R.E. Prompt

**Context:** I am a Digital Solutions Analyst informing the project team about a confirmed timeline change. The original deadline was September 15, 2026. The new deadline is September 25, 2026 because of a technical delay.

**Action:** Write a professional email explaining the timeline update.

**Rules:** Include both the old and new deadlines and briefly explain the reason. Use a professional, friendly, and concise tone. Do not blame any person or add unconfirmed impacts.

**Expected Output:** An email with a clear subject line and two short paragraphs, followed by a simple closing.

## Improved Result

**Subject: Project Timeline Update - New Deadline**

Hello Team,

Please note that the project deadline has been updated from September 15, 2026 to September 25, 2026 due to a technical delay.

Please update your plans to reflect the new date. If this change affects a confirmed dependency, kindly raise it through the project channel.

Best regards,  
Hajar

## What Improved?

- The audience and purpose are clear.
- The required dates and reason are included accurately.
- The tone and format are defined.
- The rules reduce guessing and prevent blame or invented impacts.
