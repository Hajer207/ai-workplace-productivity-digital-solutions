# Planning Workflow Example

## Goal

Prepare a fictional internal service portal for a controlled pilot within four weeks, with approved core requirements, tested critical workflows, prepared support material, and a clear go/no-go decision.

## Success Measures

- Core requirements are reviewed and approved by the appropriate stakeholders.
- Critical user journeys pass agreed acceptance criteria.
- Open high-priority risks have an owner and response plan.
- Support guidance is ready before the pilot.
- A human decision-maker approves or postpones the pilot.

## Mechanisms

1. **Requirements control:** confirm scope, priorities, assumptions, and acceptance criteria.
2. **Stakeholder alignment:** use short decision-ready updates and document decisions.
3. **Readiness tracking:** maintain actions, risks, dependencies, and evidence.
4. **Verification:** test critical workflows and check all AI-assisted documentation.

## Phases and Tasks

| Phase | Purpose | Actionable Tasks | Main Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Confirm scope | Establish a shared understanding of the pilot | Review existing notes; list confirmed and unconfirmed requirements; identify missing decisions; conduct a scope-review meeting; record approvals | Approved pilot scope and requirements list |
| 2. Prepare workflows | Make the critical service journeys testable | Map current and future workflows; draft user stories; prepare Given/When/Then acceptance criteria; review them with stakeholders | Workflow maps and approved acceptance criteria |
| 3. Validate readiness | Find gaps before the pilot | Coordinate testing; record defects; update the risk and issue log; verify support material; confirm privacy and access requirements | Readiness report with evidence and open risks |
| 4. Decide and communicate | Support a clear human go/no-go decision | Prepare a three-bullet executive brief; present open risks and options; document the decision; send an approved stakeholder update | Recorded decision and communication |

## Dependencies and Open Decisions

- Named owners and exact dates require stakeholder confirmation.
- Technical test results must be provided by the delivery team.
- Privacy and access controls require review by the responsible specialists.
- The final go/no-go decision remains with the authorized human decision-maker.
