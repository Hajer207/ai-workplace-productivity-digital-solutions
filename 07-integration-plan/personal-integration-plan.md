# Personal AI Integration Plan

The following plan describes realistic ways I can use generative AI as a Digital Solutions Analyst while keeping accuracy, confidentiality, and final decisions under human control.

| Recurring Task | Expected Benefit | Preferred Prompt or Workflow | Main Risk / Verification Requirement | Frequency | What Success Looks Like |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Turn meeting notes into a decision-ready brief | Saves time, improves consistency, and makes actions and open decisions easier to track | Use the **Decision-Ready Meeting Brief** prompt, then follow Input to Prompt to AI Output to Human Review to Final Output | AI may invent owners, deadlines, or decisions. Compare every name, date, number, decision, and action with the source | After relevant project meetings | A concise brief is ready within 20 minutes, all key facts match the source, and no invented actions remain |
| Draft stakeholder updates and project emails | Produces a clear first draft and helps adjust tone for different audiences | Use the **Timeline Change Email** or another C.A.R.E. prompt, followed by Draft to Verify to Refine to Human Sign-off | Incorrect dates, unconfirmed impacts, unsuitable tone, or accidental disclosure. Verify facts and remove sensitive details | One to three times per week, as needed | The final email is accurate, concise, audience-appropriate, and approved before sending |
| Organize requirements and draft acceptance criteria | Makes workshop notes easier to review and highlights missing information early | Use the **Requirements Extractor**, then the **Acceptance Criteria Draft** prompt after scope review | AI may add features, assumptions, or acceptance criteria not supported by stakeholders. Trace every item to source evidence and request human approval | During each requirements cycle | Requirements are traceable, gaps are visible, criteria are testable, and stakeholders confirm the final version |

## Habits I Will Build

1. Start with a clear C.A.R.E. or R.C.T.O. prompt instead of a vague request.
2. Use fictional, public, or properly anonymized information unless an approved tool and policy allow otherwise.
3. Keep the raw source beside the AI output during verification.
4. Mark missing information as `[Not Specified]` and ask a human for clarification.
5. Save useful prompts and improve them based on verified results.
6. Record human approval for important communications and decisions.
